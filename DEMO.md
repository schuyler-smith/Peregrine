# DEMO (Peregrine) — 2 minutes, 3 things

**Before you start (once):** double-click `start.bat`, make sure your key and life list are in Settings, and build the demo trip (for example 3–4 stops on a real drive). Let every stop finish loading and press **Save**. Answers are cached, so during the demo everything appears instantly.

## 1 · Plan a trip on the map (40 sec)
- Open **My trips** and open the saved trip. The pins appear in order, joined by a dashed line.
- Add one more stop live: search a town in the Itinerary tab, **or** double-click the map and choose **＋ Add stop here**. Show that nothing happens until you click its **🔍 Find birds** button, then watch its progress bar fill.
- Point out the **gold badges** on the pins. That's the number of possible lifers at each stop.

## 2 · Click a pin and see the birds there (40 sec)
- Click the pin with the biggest badge. **Stop** shows *"7 possible lifers here"* plus the county, dates and how many days of checklists it's based on.
- Tick **★ Lifers only**. The list shrinks to just the targets, most likely first, each with a likelihood bar.
- Tap ☆ on two or three target birds, then click **Find best hotspots** with 2 hotspots. Lettered markers appear on the map with what each hotspot adds. Switch to 3 hotspots to show it re-plan instantly.

## 3 · The whole trip at a glance (40 sec)
- Open the **Trip** tab: *"142 species possible · 11 lifers"*.
- With lifers only on, the numbered chips show where each target has been reported, and gold marks the best stop. Click a chip to jump there.
- Switch the likelihood filter from **Any chance** to **50%+** to show the birds you'll almost certainly see, then back again.
