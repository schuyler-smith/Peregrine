        Settings

        Welcome! Two quick things and you're ready: your free eBird API key, then (optionally) your life list.

        1 · eBird API key

        Get a free key at ebird.org/api/keygen (sign in, accept the terms, copy the key). It's stored only on this computer.

        2 · Your life list

        On eBird, open ebird.org/lifelist, choose Download (CSV), then pick that file here. (The full “Download My Data” CSV works too.) Re-import any time to update it.

        Import a route

        Paste a Google Maps directions link: in Google Maps, add all your stops, then click Share → Copy link. Or paste a list of places, one per line, like Montezuma NWR or 42.44, -76.50.

        Tour itinerary? Paste lines like Day 3: Kruger National Park. Each stop gets that day's date, and phrases like "Drive to…" are stripped out.

        …or choose a GPX, KML or KMZ file (for example from Google My Maps, Gaia GPS or AllTrails):

        When does the trip start?

        Stop 1 is on this date and each stop after it is the next day. You can change any stop's date in the Itinerary tab, and the stops after it follow on.

        Every window costs the same (about 20 eBird requests per stop). A wider window smooths out odd days; a narrower one is sharper for migration timing.
